With 260,000 verified contacts, the Indonesia Business Contact List is one of LeadsBlue's business email list datasets for Indonesia. Indonesia is Southeast Asia's largest economy and the world's 4th most populous country with 280 million people. Jakarta, the capital (being relocated to Nusantara in Borneo), is the commercial centre of a vast archipelago nation of 17,000 islands. The business ecosystem ranges from global technology companies (Gojek, Tokopedia — merged as GoTo — Traveloka, Bukalapak) to traditional conglomerates (Salim Group, Lippo Group, Sinar Mas) and a manufacturing base serving global supply chains in automotive, electronics, and textile production. ## Outreach strategy

Indonesian B2B cold email in English works for the technology, multinational, and financial services sectors. For traditional Indonesian businesses and family conglomerates, relationship-building through Indonesian networks and business associations is more effective. WhatsApp is the dominant B2B follow-up channel in Indonesia — it is far more effective than email for getting a response after an initial outreach. Jakarta's technology ecosystem (Gojek, Grab Indonesia, Tokopedia alumni) is globally connected and responsive to professional outreach.

## Compliance

Indonesia's Personal Data Protection (PDP) Law No. 27 of 2022 became effective October 2024. Implementation rules were being established in 2024-2025. Prior to the PDP Law, the ITE Law (Electronic Information and Transactions Law) and Regulation 71 on Electronic System Operations governed data handling. Enforcement of the new PDP Law is still building capacity; practical compliance risk for B2B cold email is currently low but will increase as the MCCI enforces the new framework.

## How to use this list effectively

- **Verify before sending.** B2B contact data decays roughly 2–3% per month as people change roles. Run the list through a verification pass to remove stale or invalid addresses before your first send.
- **Segment by industry and role.** Response rates improve sharply when the message matches the recipient's vertical and seniority — use the segment fields to tailor your copy rather than sending one generic message.
- **Warm up the sending domain.** Start with low daily volume from a dedicated sending domain and ramp gradually to protect deliverability.
- **Follow a multi-touch cadence.** Most replies come from the 2nd–4th touch; a short sequence over 2–3 weeks outperforms a single send.
- **Respect compliance.** Include a valid postal address and a working opt-out, and honour unsubscribe requests promptly (see compliance notes above).
