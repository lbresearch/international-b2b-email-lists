## Market overview

With 190,000 verified contacts, the Egypt Business Email Database is one of LeadsBlue's business email list datasets for Egypt. Egypt is Africa and the Arab world's most populous country (105 million people) and a significant MENA commercial hub positioned at the crossroads of Africa, the Middle East, and Europe. Cairo is the commercial capital, home to Egypt's largest banks, telecoms, and manufacturing conglomerates. Alexandria is Egypt's 2nd city and a significant industrial and port city. The Suez Canal — through which 12-15% of global trade passes — is Egypt's most important strategic economic asset and drives significant logistics, maritime, and professional services activity. Egypt's economy is diversifying from oil, tourism, and remittances toward ICT services and manufacturing under the 2030 Vision framework.

**Key business hubs:** Cairo (Smart Village, New Cairo), Alexandria, Giza, Suez, New Administrative Capital (under development).

## Outreach strategy

Egyptian B2B cold email in Arabic delivers better results for Egyptian-owned companies; English is standard for multinationals and the international business community in Cairo. Relationship-building through Egyptian business networks (American Chamber of Commerce in Egypt, British Egyptian Business Association, EgyptExport) provides credibility before direct outreach. Cairo's tech ecosystem — particularly around Smart Village and the Egyptian fintech sector — is more receptive to direct international outreach in English.

## Compliance

Egypt enacted Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020, with implementing regulations published in 2021. The law requires consent for personal data processing including marketing communications. NCIPP (National Telecom Regulatory Authority Information Protection) oversees enforcement. Enforcement capacity is still being established; practical compliance risk for B2B cold email is currently low but growing.

## How to use this list effectively

- **Verify before sending.** B2B contact data decays roughly 2–3% per month as people change roles. Run the list through a verification pass to remove stale or invalid addresses before your first send.
- **Segment by industry and role.** Response rates improve sharply when the message matches the recipient's vertical and seniority — use the segment fields to tailor your copy rather than sending one generic message.
- **Warm up the sending domain.** Start with low daily volume from a dedicated sending domain and ramp gradually to protect deliverability.
- **Follow a multi-touch cadence.** Most replies come from the 2nd–4th touch; a short sequence over 2–3 weeks outperforms a single send.
- **Respect compliance.** Include a valid postal address and a working opt-out, and honour unsubscribe requests promptly (see compliance notes above).
