## Market overview

With 770,000 verified contacts, the Vietnam Business Email Database is one of LeadsBlue's business email list datasets for Vietnam. Vietnam is one of the world's fastest-growing economies, averaging 6-7% GDP growth over the past decade, driven by export-oriented manufacturing (electronics, textiles, footwear) and a rapidly expanding services sector. Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) is the commercial capital — Vietnam's largest city and the centre of its financial, trade, and startup activity. Hanoi is the political capital with significant government-sector activity. Da Nang is a growing technology and outsourcing hub. Vietnam has become a major electronics manufacturing alternative to China, with Samsung, Apple supplier Foxconn, LG, and Intel all operating significant facilities.

**Key business hubs:** Ho Chi Minh City (Districts 1, 7), Hanoi (Ba Dinh, Cau Giay tech district), Da Nang, Binh Duong (manufacturing).

## Outreach strategy

Vietnamese B2B cold email in English works for the international business and technology sectors. Vietnamese is preferred for domestically-oriented companies. The Vietnamese startup ecosystem (VC-backed companies in Ho Chi Minh City) is increasingly internationally connected and responds to professional direct outreach. Manufacturing sector relationships typically require trade missions or in-person engagement given the complexity of supply chain negotiations.

## Compliance

Vietnam's Cybersecurity Law (2019) and Decree 13/2023 on Personal Data Protection are the primary frameworks. Consent is required for personal data processing under Decree 13. Enforcement by the Ministry of Public Security is developing; practical compliance risk for B2B cold email is currently low but regulatory attention is increasing, particularly for foreign companies.

## How to use this list effectively

- **Verify before sending.** B2B contact data decays roughly 2–3% per month as people change roles. Run the list through a verification pass to remove stale or invalid addresses before your first send.
- **Segment by industry and role.** Response rates improve sharply when the message matches the recipient's vertical and seniority — use the segment fields to tailor your copy rather than sending one generic message.
- **Warm up the sending domain.** Start with low daily volume from a dedicated sending domain and ramp gradually to protect deliverability.
- **Follow a multi-touch cadence.** Most replies come from the 2nd–4th touch; a short sequence over 2–3 weeks outperforms a single send.
- **Respect compliance.** Include a valid postal address and a working opt-out, and honour unsubscribe requests promptly (see compliance notes above).
