## Market overview

With 266,253 verified contacts, the Belarus BB Database is one of LeadsBlue's targeted email list datasets for Belarus. Belarus is a landlocked Eastern European country operating under significant international sanctions following the disputed 2020 election and the regime's support for Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine. Most Western companies have limited or eliminated Belarusian operations. Minsk was previously a significant IT hub — PandaDoc, EPAM Systems, and Wargaming (World of Tanks) were all founded or based in Minsk. Many Belarusian tech companies and professionals have relocated internationally.

**Key business hubs:** Minsk (Hi-Tech Park).

## Outreach strategy

B2B outreach into Belarus from Western companies requires sanctions screening. Most standard commercial engagement is restricted. Belarusian tech talent has largely relocated to Georgia, Poland, Lithuania, and other markets and can be accessed through those relocated companies.

## Compliance

Belarus has data protection provisions under national law but Western sanctions compliance requirements override any commercial consideration. Consult legal counsel on applicable sanctions before any engagement.

## How to use this list effectively

- **Verify before sending.** B2B contact data decays roughly 2–3% per month as people change roles. Run the list through a verification pass to remove stale or invalid addresses before your first send.
- **Segment by industry and role.** Response rates improve sharply when the message matches the recipient's vertical and seniority — use the segment fields to tailor your copy rather than sending one generic message.
- **Warm up the sending domain.** Start with low daily volume from a dedicated sending domain and ramp gradually to protect deliverability.
- **Follow a multi-touch cadence.** Most replies come from the 2nd–4th touch; a short sequence over 2–3 weeks outperforms a single send.
- **Respect compliance.** Include a valid postal address and a working opt-out, and honour unsubscribe requests promptly (see compliance notes above).
