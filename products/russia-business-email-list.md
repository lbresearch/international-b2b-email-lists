## Market overview

With 1,200,000 verified contacts, the Russia Business Email List is one of LeadsBlue's business email list datasets for Russia. Russia is the world's 11th largest economy by GDP but operates under unprecedented international sanctions since 2022. Most Western companies have reduced or eliminated Russian operations. The B2B database represents pre-sanctions Russian business contacts — the landscape has changed substantially. Moscow remains Russia's commercial capital with a significant domestic economy serving the Russian market; St. Petersburg is the 2nd commercial centre. For companies still operating in Russia or targeting domestic Russian-serving businesses, the market is large (145 million people) but complex to navigate under current sanctions frameworks.

**Key business hubs:** Moscow (Moscow City business district), St. Petersburg, Novosibirsk (tech hub), Yekaterinburg.

## Outreach strategy

B2B outreach into Russia from Western companies is subject to significant sanctions compliance requirements as of 2025. Companies must screen all Russian contacts and transactions against OFAC, EU, and UK sanctions lists before any commercial engagement. For non-sanctioned sectors (food, medicine, humanitarian), Russian-language outreach through established local partners is the most practical approach. The Russian business community that has remained internationally oriented is accessible primarily through non-Western channels and platforms.

## Compliance

Russia's Federal Law No. 152-FZ on Personal Data requires consent for personal data processing including marketing. Roskomnadzor is the enforcement authority. Russia has enacted 'data localisation' requirements — personal data of Russian citizens must be stored on servers physically located in Russia. International compliance complexity is high given sanctions overlay on top of data protection requirements.

## How to use this list effectively

- **Verify before sending.** B2B contact data decays roughly 2–3% per month as people change roles. Run the list through a verification pass to remove stale or invalid addresses before your first send.
- **Segment by industry and role.** Response rates improve sharply when the message matches the recipient's vertical and seniority — use the segment fields to tailor your copy rather than sending one generic message.
- **Warm up the sending domain.** Start with low daily volume from a dedicated sending domain and ramp gradually to protect deliverability.
- **Follow a multi-touch cadence.** Most replies come from the 2nd–4th touch; a short sequence over 2–3 weeks outperforms a single send.
- **Respect compliance.** Include a valid postal address and a working opt-out, and honour unsubscribe requests promptly (see compliance notes above).
