## Market overview

With 290,000 verified contacts, the Romania Business Email Database is one of LeadsBlue's business email list datasets for Romania. Romania is Central-Eastern Europe's 2nd largest market after Poland, with a 19-million-person population and one of the EU's fastest economic growth rates. Bucharest is the commercial capital — a rapidly modernising city hosting the regional offices of many multinationals and a fast-growing technology sector. Cluj-Napoca has become Romania's technology capital — home to a world-class IT industry that produces more software developers per capita than almost any EU city, attracting offices from major global tech companies. Timișoara is a significant automotive and manufacturing hub near the Serbian border.

**Key business hubs:** Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca (tech), Timișoara (automotive), Iași (IT outsourcing), Brasov.

## Compliance

Romania follows GDPR, enforced by ANSPDCP (Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal). ANSPDCP applies legitimate interest for B2B cold email to corporate addresses where professionally relevant. Romania's implementation of GDPR is standard EU practice; ANSPDCP is a moderately active enforcement authority.

## How to use this list effectively

- **Verify before sending.** B2B contact data decays roughly 2–3% per month as people change roles. Run the list through a verification pass to remove stale or invalid addresses before your first send.
- **Segment by industry and role.** Response rates improve sharply when the message matches the recipient's vertical and seniority — use the segment fields to tailor your copy rather than sending one generic message.
- **Warm up the sending domain.** Start with low daily volume from a dedicated sending domain and ramp gradually to protect deliverability.
- **Follow a multi-touch cadence.** Most replies come from the 2nd–4th touch; a short sequence over 2–3 weeks outperforms a single send.
- **Respect compliance.** Include a valid postal address and a working opt-out, and honour unsubscribe requests promptly (see compliance notes above).
